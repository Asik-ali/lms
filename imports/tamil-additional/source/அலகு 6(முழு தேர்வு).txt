TAMIL NADU PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION 
 
Office Order No. 168, Date 12.03.2021 
 
Subject: Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Closely Examined Procedures - Reg. 
 
(1) The following order is issued regarding the close examination of the Tamil Nadu Government 
Gazette and its sending for the perusal of the officer. 
 
(2) The responsibility of sending the Tamil Nadu Government Gazette to the officials lies with the 
Department of registrations and Forms. Once the Tamil Nadu Government Gazette is received 
from the Government Printing Office from post office, it should be sent to the Department of 
registrations and Forms Division. The Government Gazette should be arranged in the order of 
its parts, for example, Part-1, Section-1, Part-II, Section-2, then Part-II, Section-1, 2, etc., then 
its links should be arranged as Part-III and then stitched. The Government Gazette should be 
sent to the Assistant or Assistant Division Officer of the Department of registrations and Forms. 
Division after a close examination of whether the notifications related to this office have been 
published and sent to the Sub-Secretary. The Sub-Secretary should send the Government 
Gazette to the Secretary and the Selection Committee for their inspection. 
 
(3) The Tamil Nadu Government Gazette, upon its return from the field, shall be divided into 
separate parts by the Assistant Divisional Officer of the Department of registrations and Forms, 
Division with the help of the Registrar in the Registry Department, each part of the Government 
Gazette with its annexes, for easy perusal. In the months of January and July, each part of the 
Government Gazette for the previous half-year shall be sent to the Government Printing Office 
for binding. The bound Government Gazettes shall be kept in a safe place for easy perusal 
when required. A record in this regard shall be maintained by the Assistant or Assistant 
Divisional Officer of the Department of registrations and Forms Division, indicating the reports 
and news relating to the Election Commission with the part and page numbers respectively.
