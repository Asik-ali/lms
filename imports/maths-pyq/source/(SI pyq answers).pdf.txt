Question 1: Find the number of years for the principal Rs. 2,500, rate of interest 2.5% and simple interest Rs. 187.50?
Options: (A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 5
Correct Answer: (B) 3 (3 years)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Formula: T = (SI × 100) / (P × R)
Calculation: T = (187.50 × 100) / (2500 × 2.5) = 18750 / 6250 = 3 years.
Question 2: A principal becomes Rs. 17,000 at the rate of 12% in 3 years. Find the principal amount.
Options: (A) Rs. 14,000 (B) Rs. 12,500 (C) Rs. 15,200 (D) Rs. 13,500
Correct Answer: (B) Rs. 12,500 (Rs. 12,500)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Formula: A = P[1 + (R × T)/100] => 17000 = P[1 + (12 × 3)/100] = P(1.36)
Calculation: P = 17000 / 1.36 = Rs. 12,500.
Question 3: Ram deposited Rs. 200 at the beginning of every month in a Recurring Deposit (R.D.) and received Rs.
19,656 at the end of 6 years. Find the rate of Simple Interest paid by the bank.
Options: (A) 8% (B) 10% (C) 12% (D) 9%
Correct Answer: (C) 12% (12%)
Step-by-Step Solution:
n = 6 × 12 = 72 months. Total deposited = 72 × 200 = Rs. 14,400.
Interest = 19656 - 14400 = Rs. 5256.
Equivalent Principal P_eq = n(n+1)/2 × 200 = (72 × 73 / 2) × 200 = 525,600.
Interest = (P_eq × R) / 1200 => 5256 = (525600 × R) / 1200 = 438 R => R = 12%.
Question 4: What sum of money will earn Rs. 500 as simple interest in 1 year at 5% per annum?
Options: (A) 50,000 (B) 30,000 (C) 10,000 (D) 5,000
Correct Answer: (C) 10,000 (Rs. 10,000)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Formula: P = (SI × 100) / (R × T)
Calculation: P = (500 × 100) / (5 × 1) = 50000 / 5 = Rs. 10,000.
Question 5: A person decides to create a fund to be invested at 10% per annum to provide for a prize of Rs. 500 every
year. The initial investment is:
Options: (A) Rs. 4,000 (B) Rs. 5,000 (C) Rs. 6,000 (D) Rs. 5,500
Correct Answer: (B) Rs. 5,000 (Rs. 5,000)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Annual Interest = Rs. 500 at R = 10% p.a.
P = (SI × 100) / (R × T) = (500 × 100) / (10 × 1) = Rs. 5,000.

Question 6: A sum of Rs. 46,000 was lent out at Simple Interest and at the end of 1 year and 9 months, the total
amount was Rs. 52,440. Find the rate of interest per year.
Options: (A) 5% (B) 6% (C) 7% (D) 8%
Correct Answer: (D) 8% (8%)
Step-by-Step Solution:
SI = 52440 - 46000 = Rs. 6440. T = 1 + 9/12 = 1.75 years.
R = (SI × 100) / (P × T) = (6440 × 100) / (46000 × 1.75) = 644000 / 80500 = 8%.
Question 7: Find the principal on amount Rs. 11,800 at 6% per annum for 3 years being simple interest annually.
Options: (A) Rs. 8,000 (B) Rs. 9,000 (C) Rs. 10,000 (D) Rs. 9,500
Correct Answer: (C) Rs. 10,000 (Rs. 10,000)
Step-by-Step Solution:
A = P(1 + RT/100) => 11800 = P(1 + 18/100) = 1.18 P
P = 11800 / 1.18 = Rs. 10,000.
Question 8: A sum of Rs. 48,000 was lent out at simple interest and at the end of 2 years and 3 months the total
amount was Rs. 55,560. Find the rate of interest per year.
Options: (A) 7% (B) 8% (C) 9% (D) 10%
Correct Answer: (A) 7% (7%)
Step-by-Step Solution:
SI = 55560 - 48000 = Rs. 7560. T = 2 + 3/12 = 2.25 years (9/4 yrs).
R = (SI × 100) / (P × T) = (7560 × 100) / (48000 × 2.25) = 756000 / 108000 = 7%.
Question 9: Sathish Kumar bought an amount of Rs. 52,000 from a person on simple interest. After 4 years he paid
the total amount Rs. 79,040 to the person. Calculate the rate of simple interest.

Correct Answer: (C) 13% (13%)
Step-by-Step Solution:
SI = 79040 - 52000 = Rs. 27,040.
R = (27040 × 100) / (52000 × 4) = 2704000 / 208000 = 13%.
Question 10: Find the principal that will yield a simple interest of Rs. 300 in 3 years at 2% rate of interest per annum.

P = (SI × 100) / (R × T) = (300 × 100) / (2 × 3) = 30000 / 6 = Rs. 5,000.
Question 11: At what rate of simple interest a certain sum will double in 15 years?
Options: (A) 6 1/3 % (B) 5 1/3 % (C) 5 2/3 % (D) 6 2/3 %

same rate of interest.

Question 14: Find the simple interest on Rs. 5x^2 y^2 for 4xy years at 7y% per annum.
Options: (A) (7/5) x^3 y^4 (B) (7/4) x^2 y^3 (C) (4/5) x^3 y^2 (D) (4/7) x^2 y^4
Correct Answer: (A) (7/5) x^3 y^4 ((7/5) x^3 y^4)
Step-by-Step Solution:
SI = (P × R × T) / 100 = (5 x^2 y^2 × 7y × 4xy) / 100 = (140 x^3 y^4) / 100 = (7/5) x^3 y^4.
Question 15: Value of a machine depreciates 10% p.a. If the present value is Rs. 16,000, after how many years will it
be worth half the present value? (Note: In standard simple depreciation / exam key context)
Options: (A) 2 years (B) 3 years (C) 4 years (D) 5 years
Correct Answer: (D) 5 years (5 years)
Step-by-Step Solution:
Half present value = 50% loss. Under linear depreciation of 10% p.a.:
Time = 50% / 10% = 5 years.

Total Amount A = 20000 + 4500 = Rs. 24,500.
Question 17: The difference between the simple interest received from two different sources on Rs. 1,500 for 3 years
is Rs. 13.50. The difference between their rate of interest is:
Options: (A) 0.1% (B) 0.2% (C) 0.3% (D) 0.4%
Correct Answer: (C) 0.3% (0.3%)
Step-by-Step Solution:
SI1 - SI2 = P × T × (R1 - R2) / 100 => 13.50 = (1500 × 3 × ∆ R) / 100 = 45 ∆ R
∆ R = 13.50 / 45 = 0.3%.
